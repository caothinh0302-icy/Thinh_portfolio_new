PROJECT 01 / MECHATRONICS

Prosthetic Hand (Servo-Actuated)

Built & Tested

As part of the Phoenix Hand open-source prosthetic initiative, I designed and built a tendon-driven prosthetic hand using a single high-torque servo (MG946R) to actuate all five fingers through a shared tendon-routing system, with Force-Sensitive Resistors (FSRs) providing haptic feedback for grip control. I designed a custom hand guard and motor casing in SolidWorks to protect the internal servo components while preserving full finger range of motion.

Design Objectives

  • Protect the high-torque servo motor from mechanical stress and impact
  • Maintain full finger flexion range of motion without restricting tendon paths
  • Achieve a compact, wearable form factor suitable for daily use
  • Design for 3D printability using standard FDM processes

Tools & Techniques

  • SolidWorks: part modeling, assembly constraints, dimensional verification
  • FDM 3D printing for prototype fabrication
  • Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) applied to mechanical stress points, improving grip reliability by 33%
  • Closed-loop Arduino Nano control architecture using FSR feedback for non-destructive object manipulation
